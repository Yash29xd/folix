Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Your files

PDFs you open are processed on your device by your browser. They are not uploaded, stored or shared by us.

Data we collect

Folix does not use accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not collect personal data itself.

Saved in your browser

Folix stores your settings, annotations and last-read page for each document in your browser's local storage, keyed to a fingerprint of the file rather than its name. A signature you create is stored the same way. Folix also keeps a copy of the app in your browser's cache so it can work offline. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Third parties

  • Hosting: the site is served from GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and user agent under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Nothing else: the PDF engine, fonts and icons are bundled with the site, so Folix makes no requests to other companies' servers.

Opening a file by URL

If you use ?file=, your browser fetches that URL directly from the address you provided.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision. Questions: contact.
