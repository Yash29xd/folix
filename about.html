About Folix

Last updated:

Folix is a fast, lightweight PDF viewer that runs entirely in your browser. It is a product.

What it does

  • Continuous scrolling, zoom, fit-width and page navigation
  • Text selection, copy and in-document search
  • Password-protected PDFs, page thumbnails and outline
  • Annotations: pen, marker, highlight, notes, undo and save as PDF
  • Double-tap zoom on phones and resume on your last page
  • Works offline and installs as an app
  • Light, sepia and dark themes, a spotlight night-reading mode, and a two-page view
  • Text boxes and signatures
  • Download and print
  • Works on desktop and mobile, including pinch-to-zoom

How it works

Folix uses Mozilla's open-source PDF.js to read and draw pages. Only the pages near your screen are rendered, which keeps memory use low on phones.

Roadmap

Form filling and page tools such as reorder and merge are planned.
