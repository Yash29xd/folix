FAQ

Last updated:

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is opened locally in your browser and never sent to a server.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit Folix keeps a copy in your browser and works without internet. Installing it as an app makes that even smoother.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Folix asks for the password when you open the file. Saving annotations into a locked PDF is not supported.

Why is a very large PDF slow?

Complex pages take time to draw. Zooming out or using a desktop browser helps.

Can I annotate PDFs?

Yes. Open Notes for pen, marker, text highlight, sticky notes and an eraser. Annotations are saved in your browser for each document, and Save PDF downloads a copy with them included.

Does it remember where I stopped?

Yes. Folix reopens a document on the last page you read, on the same device and browser.

How do I install Folix?

In Chrome or Edge use the Install app button on the home screen, or the install icon in the address bar. On iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen.

Can Folix be my default PDF app?

On Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS with Chrome or Edge, an installed Folix appears under Open with for PDF files, and you can pick it as the default in your system settings. On Android, installed Folix appears in the Share menu, so share a PDF from Files, Drive or WhatsApp and choose Folix. A website cannot set itself as the default by itself, and iPhone does not allow web apps to open PDFs.

Can I fill in PDF forms?

Yes. Form fields (text boxes, checkboxes, drop-downs) are clickable and editable right on the page. Save PDF or Share include what you typed. Links inside PDFs work too.

How does search work now?

Type in the search box. You get a count, previous and next buttons, exact highlights, and a Find tab listing every match with its surrounding text. Click the count to open the list.

Can Folix read a PDF aloud?

Yes. Press V or use the speaker button. It reads from the current page, highlights the sentence being read and turns pages for you. Select some text first to read just that. It uses your device's voices, so quality depends on them.

Can I rearrange pages or merge PDFs?

Yes. The Page tools button lets you drag pages into a new order, rotate or delete them, add pages from another PDF, extract selected pages and save a copy. Your annotations are included in the result.

Can I sign a PDF?

Yes. Open Notes, choose Sign, draw or type your signature, then click the page where it goes. You can drag it afterwards. Save PDF includes it in the copy you download.

How do I share a PDF?

Use the Share button in the toolbar. On Android it opens the same share sheet as other apps, so you can send the file to WhatsApp, email, Drive and more. If you added annotations, the shared copy includes them. Browsers without file sharing download the PDF instead.

Can I change the look of the whole site?

Yes. The theme button (half-filled circle) cycles Light, Sepia and Dark for the entire site, and Folix remembers your choice.

What is spotlight mode?

A night-reading mode. The page goes dark and only a soft circle around your mouse or finger is lit, so you read one small area at a time. Turn it on with the sun button or the L key, and change the circle size with [ and ]. It also works on top of the dark theme.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Press ? in the viewer to see them all.

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact page and include your browser, device and, if possible, a sample PDF that is safe to share.
